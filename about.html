About PetCalcHub

Free, science-backed pet calculators with AI-powered health insights — now with 5 emergency toxicity tools.

Our Mission

PetCalcHub was built with a simple belief: every pet parent deserves access to reliable, science-backed tools to make informed decisions about their pet's health — without paywalls, signups, or data collection.

We combine veterinary-standard formulas from trusted sources — including AAHA, AAFCO, NRC, and Merck Veterinary Manual — with AI-powered insights that personalize each result for your specific pet. The result is a tool that doesn't just give you a number; it helps you understand what that number means and what to do next.

Vet-Reviewed Content

Every calculator formula and AI-generated care tip on PetCalcHub is reviewed for clinical accuracy by a licensed veterinarian. Our content follows Google's E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) principles.

Emergency Tools — Our Biggest Priority

"Dog ate chocolate" is one of the highest-searched pet emergencies on the internet. We built 5 dedicated emergency toxicity calculators so pet parents can assess risk in seconds — not minutes — when every moment counts:

  • Chocolate Toxicity — theobromine dose based on chocolate type & dog weight
  • Grape & Raisin Toxicity — kidney failure risk assessment
  • Xylitol Toxicity — hypoglycemia & liver failure risk from sugar-free products
  • Onion & Garlic Toxicity — Heinz body anemia risk from Allium family foods
  • Ibuprofen (Advil) Toxicity — GI ulcer, kidney, and CNS toxicity risk

Each emergency calculator includes ASPCA Poison Control and Pet Poison Helpline numbers, step-by-step action guidance, and AI-generated advice tailored to the calculated risk level.
